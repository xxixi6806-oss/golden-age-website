HOW WE WORK

A considered approach
to every enquiry.

We believe professional communication should be accurate, documented where appropriate and aligned with the rules that apply to the subject and market.

INFORMATION & DOCUMENTATION

Accuracy matters.
Context matters too.

Documents and statements should always be interpreted in their proper context. A document alone does not establish regulatory approval, suitability, safety, efficacy or permission to supply.

  • Use current, attributable information.
  • Check the scope and date of any document.
  • Verify claims with the relevant issuing organisation or authority.
  • Assess regulatory requirements independently in each market.
