This page outlines the information and verification principles relevant to professional and organisational discussions. It is not a product catalogue or an offer to supply therapeutic goods.

A clear framework

What should be established first

Before any regulated activity is considered, the relevant parties should establish the legal status, authorisations and permitted scope in each destination market.

01

Regulatory status

Confirm product classification, applicable registrations and the permissions required for the intended activity in the destination jurisdiction.

02

Supporting records

Review relevant records and source documents. A document should be checked for issuer, scope, date, batch relevance and independent verifiability.

03

Supply-chain review

Understand the parties, responsibilities, import requirements and record-keeping obligations involved in any permitted supply chain.

Documentation principles

Evidence over assumptions.

A document or certificate should never be treated as proof of every quality, safety, regulatory or clinical claim. Its meaning depends on the issuer, method, scope and material tested.

  • Verify the issuing organisation independently
  • Check dates, scope and identifying details
  • Confirm that records relate to the relevant material or batch
  • Seek independent professional and regulatory advice where required
